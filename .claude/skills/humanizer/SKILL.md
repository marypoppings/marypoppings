# Humanizer

Edit draft text so it sounds like the person or brand voice it's for. This improves writing quality; the text is still posted by the user under their own name, and nothing here is for hiding that automation is used on the platform.

## Process

1. Get the voice: read the account's `voice` field (or ask for 2–3 samples of the user's real writing). Match its vocabulary, punctuation, emoji habits, and length.
2. Find the specific hook: a detail from the post or situation (a place, a number, a moment). If the draft has none, add one or flag that one is needed. Never invent facts about the post you haven't seen.
3. Cut the AI tells below, then read it aloud: would this person actually type it?
4. Vary the output. Across a batch, change length, opening word, structure, and emoji use. No two comments should share a template.

## AI tells to remove

- Openers: "Great post!", "Absolutely love this!", "What a fantastic…", "This is so inspiring!"
- Filler and hype words: "game-changer", "dive into", "delve", "unlock", "elevate", "journey", "vibrant", "tapestry", "ever-evolving", "in today's world"
- Structure: tidy three-item lists, "not just X, but Y", rhetorical-question hooks, a bow-tying closing line, em dashes everywhere
- Over-polish: perfect punctuation in casual contexts, hashtags in comments, exclamation marks on every sentence, a stack of identical emoji
- Vague praise with no detail, or questions that could be asked of any post

## Do instead

- Say one real, specific thing: a reaction, a tiny opinion, a related experience, a genuine question.
- Short and imperfect is fine: fragments, lowercase, a contraction, a single emoji if the account uses them.
- Comments: 4–25 words. Captions: lead with the point, not a setup. Replies: answer the question first.
- Keep claims honest. No fake personal anecdotes, fake testimonials, or invented stats.

## Output

Return the revised text only (for batches, a numbered list), plus a one-line note if you changed meaning or something is missing (e.g. "needs a specific detail from the video").
